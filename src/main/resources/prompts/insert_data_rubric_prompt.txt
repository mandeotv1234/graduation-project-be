Ban la chuyen gia cham diem SQL cho he thong thi thuc hanh CSDL.
Phan tich SQL INSERT ben duoi va tao rubric theo KIEU TRU DIEM.

=== CAU HOI ===
%s

=== SQL DAP AN MAU ===
%s

=== TONG DIEM ===
%.2f diem

=== DAU RA BAT BUOC ===
Tra ve DUNG JSON (khong markdown, khong giai thich):
{
  "total_points": %.2f,
  "question_category": "INSERT_DATA",
  "grading_rules": [
    {
      "rule_name": "<TEN_RULE>",
      "target": "<TABLE|COLUMN|DATA_TYPE|PRIMARY_KEY|FOREIGN_KEY|CONSTRAINT_LOCAL|COLUMN_ORDER|ROW|CELL_VALUE|ROW_ORDER>",
      "condition": "<IS_MISSING|IS_EXTRA|IS_NULL|NOT_EQUAL|OUT_OF_ORDER|TYPE_MISMATCH|LENGTH_MISMATCH|REFERENCE_ERROR>",
      "modifiers": ["<TO_LOWERCASE|TRIM_WHITESPACE|REMOVE_ALL_WHITESPACE|REMOVE_DIACRITICS|REMOVE_SPECIAL_CHARS|CAST_TO_STRING|CAST_TO_FLOAT|ROUND_TO_INT|ROUND_2_DECIMALS|MATCH_FAMILY_TYPE|SORT_ASC>"],

=== QUY TAC QUAN TRONG ===
- Day la rubric TRU DIEM, KHONG phai rubric cong diem.
- Dung rule_name (KHONG dung rule_id).
- Cho phep "rule dac biet" mo ta bang text. Rule dac biet toi thieu:
  {
    "rule_name": "<TEN_RULE>",
    "description": "<MO_TA_QUY_TAC_DAC_BIET_BANG_NGON_NGU_TU_NHIEN>"
  }
- Rule dac biet co the kem action/penalty_value neu muon tru diem truc tiep.
- tables co cau truc LINH HOAT: cac field con trong table duoc phep co hoac khong tuy ngu canh.
- Neu rule yeu cau dieu chinh doi so khop du lieu, duoc phep cap nhat columns_config, expected_data, match_type, missing_row_penalty...
- KHONG tao cac khoi cu sau trong JSON moi:
  - grading_payload.grading_settings
  - allow_extra_rows
  - penalty_per_extra_row
  - expected_datasets

=== NGUYEN TAC PHAN BO DIEM ===
- Neu co table_points thi tong table_points nen xap xi %.2f (lech toi da 0.01 do lam tron).
- Neu bai can cham theo quy tac tong quat va khong can chi tiet tung bang, co the bo qua table_points.
- penalty_value phai >= 0.

=== KIEM TRA TRUOC KHI TRA VE ===
- JSON hop le, chi tra ve JSON.
- Co question_category = INSERT_DATA.
- Co grading_rules (it nhat 1 rule).
- Moi rule deu co rule_name.
- Neu co tables thi moi phan tu phai co table_name.
