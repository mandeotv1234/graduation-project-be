=== BAT BUOC TRA VE ===
- Chi tra ve JSON hop le. Khong markdown. Khong giai thich.
- Dung dung cau truc JSON ben duoi.
- Phai co day du tat ca bang, tat ca cot, va tat ca PK/FK xuat hien trong SQL.
- Khong duoc bo cot thuong (khong chi giu lai PK/FK).
- Neu SQL co UNIQUE/CHECK/DEFAULT thi dua vao constraints.
- Khong can toi uu phan bo diem. He thong BE se tu dien penalty mac dinh sau.

=== LUU Y QUAN TRONG ===
- AI chi can tra ve dung cau truc va noi dung ky thuat (bang/cot/kieu/nullability/constraints).
- Cac truong penalty co the de so placeholder (vi du 0 hoac 1).
- BE se tu dong ghi de penalty theo rule mac dinh:
  missing_column_penalty = 0.5
  type_mismatch_penalty = 0.25
  missing_constraint_penalty = 0.5 cho PRIMARY_KEY va FOREIGN_KEY
  missing_table_penalty duoc BE phan bo theo tong diem cau hoi
